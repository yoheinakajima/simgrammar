### 5.4 Families of games by what they use

Clustering games by the mechanics they use, ignoring their subgenre labels, gives {{stat:n_clusters}} clusters, {{stat:n_clusters_cross_subgenre}} of which mix subgenres.{{assert:n_clusters_cross_subgenre >= 10}} Each mechanic is weighted by inverse prevalence, so two games sharing a rare mechanic count as closer than two games sharing a pause button.

The number of clusters is not tuned to the result. The build re-clusters {{stat:cluster_subsamples}} subsamples, each holding about {{stat:cluster_subsample_pct}} percent of the games, and compares each with the full clustering by adjusted Rand index (ARI). A game's membership in each subsample is fixed by a hash of its id, so adding or removing a game leaves the other games' subsamples unchanged. The rule takes the smallest k whose mean ARI reaches {{stat:cluster_min_mean_ari}}. When it was last applied it selected k = {{stat:cluster_k}} (mean ARI now {{stat:cluster_mean_ari}}), and that k is fixed in `report/analysis.yaml` so that adding a game does not reshuffle every cluster. On the current data the rule would select k = {{stat:cluster_rule_k}}; the table marks both. The validator warns only when the two differ by more than resampling noise ({{stat:cluster_drift_tolerance}} in mean ARI), and they do not. The curve is that flat: a few added games moved the rule's pick by several clusters. Neighbouring choices of k are therefore about as defensible as the configured one, and what matters is which groupings survive resampling. {{stat:n_stable_clusters}} of the {{stat:n_clusters}} clusters keep a mean Jaccard overlap of at least {{stat:cluster_stable_jaccard}} with their best match across subsamples (the stability column below). When this section says games belong together, they share a cluster in at least {{stat:cocluster_together_pct}} percent of subsamples; when it says they stand apart, in at most {{stat:cocluster_apart_pct}} percent. Weaker links are described as weaker and pinned by comparison. The build fails if any of these stops holding.

The clusters follow subgenre lines only in part. Their agreement with subgenre labels is ARI {{stat:cluster_subgenre_ari}} and normalized mutual information {{stat:cluster_subgenre_nmi}}, against a mean of {{stat:cluster_subgenre_null_nmi}} when subgenre labels are shuffled across games ({{stat:cluster_permutations}} permutations, p = {{stat:p_cluster_subgenre_nmi}} for NMI and {{stat:p_cluster_subgenre_ari}} for ARI).{{assert:cluster_subgenre_ari < 0.6}}{{assert:cluster_subgenre_nmi > cluster_subgenre_null_nmi}}{{assert:p_cluster_subgenre_nmi <= 0.01}}{{assert:p_cluster_subgenre_ari <= 0.01}} That null mean shows how much of the raw NMI is chance; corrected for it, adjusted mutual information is {{stat:cluster_subgenre_ami}}, on the same scale as ARI.{{assert:cluster_subgenre_ami < cluster_subgenre_nmi}}{{assert:cluster_subgenre_ami > 0.2}}{{assert:cluster_subgenre_ami < 0.8}} Mechanics recover lineage far better than chance and still cut across it often.

### 5.6 The legibility frontier

Across all mechanics, legibility and emergence potential are negatively correlated (Spearman ρ = {{stat:rho_legibility_emergence_potential}}, p {{stat:p_legibility_emergence_potential}}), and emergence potential is strongly correlated with tuning difficulty (ρ = {{stat:rho_emergence_potential_tuning_difficulty}}, p {{stat:p_emergence_potential_tuning_difficulty}}). Mechanics rated as generating more unscripted variety are also rated harder to understand and harder to balance.{{assert:rho_legibility_emergence_potential < -0.2}}{{assert:rho_emergence_potential_tuning_difficulty > 0.2}}{{assert:rho_legibility_compute_cost < 0}} Legibility also falls as compute cost rises (ρ = {{stat:rho_legibility_compute_cost}}, p {{stat:p_legibility_compute_cost}}): expensive simulation tends to be hidden simulation.

Two caveats bound these numbers. First, the original rubric scores were assigned by one model rater (claude-opus-5-5) against written anchors. Independent second ratings now cover {{stat:n_scores_independently_rated}} scores from {{stat:n_mechanics_independently_rated}} mechanics, and the remaining scores retain their original values.{{assert:n_scores_independently_rated > 0}} On the rated mechanics, the two raters chose the same anchor for {{stat:pct_rater_exact_tuning_difficulty}} percent of tuning-difficulty scores and {{stat:pct_rater_exact_emergence_potential}} percent of emergence scores, the two scales of the strongest correlation, against {{stat:pct_rater_exact_legibility}} percent for legibility (`dist/analysis/rater-agreement.csv`).{{assert:pct_rater_exact_tuning_difficulty < pct_rater_exact_legibility}}{{assert:pct_rater_exact_emergence_potential < pct_rater_exact_legibility}} On the {{stat:n_mechanics_second_rated_legibility_emergence}} mechanics whose legibility and emergence scores both have a second rating, the legibility-emergence correlation is ρ = {{stat:rho_legibility_emergence_potential_second_rater}} on the second rater's scores and ρ = {{stat:rho_legibility_emergence_potential_original_rater}} on the original rater's scores for the same mechanics, both negative; that n is too small to confirm or refute the correlation across the full set.{{assert:n_mechanics_second_rated_legibility_emergence >= 5}}{{assert:rho_legibility_emergence_potential_second_rater < 0}}{{assert:rho_legibility_emergence_potential_original_rater < 0}}{{assert:p_legibility_emergence_potential_second_rater > 0.05}}{{assert:p_legibility_emergence_potential_original_rater > 0.05}} The correlations largely describe the original rater's reading of design knowledge. The p-values test association across mechanics; they do not measure agreement between raters or player behavior. Second, mechanics in the legibility and time-and-pacing families sit at the bottom of the emergence scale by construction ({{stat:pct_interface_emergence_1}} percent score 1), since an overlay or a pause button generates nothing on its own; excluding them{{assert:pct_interface_emergence_1 >= 50}} leaves the legibility-emergence correlation at ρ = {{stat:rho_legibility_emergence_potential_excl_interface}}, weaker but the same sign.{{assert:rho_legibility_emergence_potential_excl_interface < 0}}{{assert:rho_legibility_emergence_potential_excl_interface > rho_legibility_emergence_potential}}
